My Story

Building through experience

My name is Carlos Gomez, and I am based in Austin, Texas. I am currently serving in the U.S. Army Reserve as an Intelligence Analyst while continuing my education in Computing Applications. My path has not been limited to one field, and that is something I have learned to value.

Before entering military intelligence, I developed professional experience in both security operations and team leadership. Working in high-rise security taught me to pay attention to the environment around me, document incidents accurately, communicate with different types of people, and remain professional when responding to unexpected situations.

Earlier in my career, I worked as a team leader in a fast-paced retail operation. Managing employees, workflow, customer issues, and daily priorities helped me understand that leadership is often less about having a title and more about setting expectations, solving problems, and keeping people moving in the same direction.

My Army experience added another layer to that foundation. Intelligence work strengthened my interest in analysis, information management, threat awareness, reporting, and decision support. It confirmed that I enjoy work where information has to be organized, evaluated, and communicated clearly.
