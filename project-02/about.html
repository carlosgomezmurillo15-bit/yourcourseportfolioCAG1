About Me

Different experiences. One professional direction.

My background combines military intelligence, technology, security operations, leadership, education, and a strong interest in public service. I see each of these experiences as part of the same larger goal: becoming someone who can understand difficult problems, communicate clearly, and contribute to meaningful decisions.

My Story

Building through experience.

My name is Carlos Gomez, and I am based in Austin, Texas. I am currently studying Computing Applications at Texas Tech University while serving in the U.S. Army Reserve as an Intelligence Analyst.

My path has developed through several different environments, and each one has taught me something valuable. Working in security operations taught me to pay attention to details, maintain situational awareness, document incidents accurately, communicate professionally, and remain calm when situations become unpredictable.

Earlier in my professional experience, I worked in team leadership in a fast-paced retail operation. Managing people, workflow, customer concerns, and daily performance helped me understand that leadership is often about consistency more than authority. People need clear expectations, communication, accountability, and someone willing to solve problems when things do not go according to plan.

My Army experience strengthened my interest in intelligence and analysis. I became more interested in how information is collected, organized, evaluated, and communicated. I enjoy work where the goal is to take a complicated situation and identify what information matters most.

My education in Computing Applications is helping me expand that analytical mindset into technology. I want to understand not only how information is interpreted, but also how digital systems, data, and technical tools can make analytical work more effective.
