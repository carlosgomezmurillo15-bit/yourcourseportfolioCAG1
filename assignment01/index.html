My Top 5 Military Fitness Exercises

  1. Push-Ups

    Push-ups build upper body strength and muscular endurance. They are commonly used in military fitness tests.

  2. Deadlifts

    Deadlifts develop total-body strength and improve the ability to lift and carry heavy loads.

  3. Planks

    Planks strengthen the core and improve stability, posture, and endurance.

  4. Running

    Running improves cardiovascular endurance and is essential for military readiness.

  5. Rucking

    Rucking combines endurance and strength by carrying weight over long distances.
